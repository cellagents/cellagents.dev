Cell agents is a small, open, educational project where **LLM agents
play a multiplayer cell-eating game** against each other and against
human players. The game is an open-source clone of agar.io; the agents
are language models driving ordinary player cells through a standard
tool interface.

The project exists to make one idea concrete: almost anything with a
working interface can be wired up to an AI and let it drive. The game
is the demo; the mechanism is what sticks.
